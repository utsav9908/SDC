About e-Rupee

e-Rupee is a digital voucher-based payment solution. It was developed by NPCI. It helps deliver benefits for specific purposes.

Features

  • Cashless payment
  • Contactless process
  • Purpose-specific vouchers
  • Convenient payment system

How It Works

  1. A digital voucher is issued.
  2. The beneficiary receives the voucher.
  3. The voucher is used for its intended purpose.

Advantages

  • Easy voucher-based payments
  • Helps deliver benefits
  • Reduces physical paperwork
  • Supports transparent transactions

Disadvantages

  • Vouchers have limited uses
  • Not all merchants may accept them
  • Technical problems may occur
  • Some people may need help using them
